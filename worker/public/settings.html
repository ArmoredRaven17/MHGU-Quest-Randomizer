How this works

This page lets you configure the quest pool your Twitch chat bot rolls from — instead of every streamer sharing one fixed pool, you get your own, saved under your Twitch login.

1. Configure filters below. Quest Filters, Monsters, Weapons, Styles, Hunter Arts, Restrictions, Challenges, and Prowler work the same way as the main site's checkboxes — uncheck anything to exclude it from your pool.

2. Preview, then Save & Publish. Preview Roll shows what a roll looks like with your current settings without saving anything. Save & Publish is what actually updates your channel's live bot commands.

3. Grab your bot commands. Click "Bot Commands" above for the exact strings to paste into your chat bot — a full-roll command, plus quest-only and weapon-only variants in case you want separate commands for each.

Export/Import saves your filters to a file, or loads one back — including files exported from the main GitHub Pages site, since both use the same format.

Your settings are tied to your Twitch login, not this browser — log in again from any device and they'll be there.
